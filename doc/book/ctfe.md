## ctfe-seriesTex
Two graphs: how long a build took, and the most memory it held. A line a place it was built, so a hosted runner is never read against a laptop. Each graph is its own paragraph, unindented, so neither runs on from the lead above it. A tick says the minute as well as the day, since a day holds many builds, and the ticks stand far enough apart that no two labels touch. The legend sits under the axis, where it covers no point.
